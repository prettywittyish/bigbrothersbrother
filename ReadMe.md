# Big Brother’s Brother (B³) AI Corruption Watchdog System

## Overview
**Big Brother’s Brother (B³)** is an AI-driven watchdog system designed to detect and expose **financial corruption** in government. It monitors insider trading, dark money, conflicts of interest, and lobbying influence, ensuring **real-time transparency** in political finance and decision-making.

## Key AI Modules
- 📉 **StockShark AI** – Tracks insider trading & suspicious stock movements.  
- 💰 **KickbackTracker** – Detects bribes & shady government contracts.  
- 🕵️ **DarkMoney Detector** – Follows hidden campaign finance deals.  
- 📜 **VoteGuard AI** – Flags votes that financially benefit lawmakers.  
- 🐊 **SwampDrain AI** – Tracks lobbyist influence & policy changes.  
- 🔗 **TruthLedger** – Uses blockchain for full financial transparency.  
- 👥 **Nepotism Net** – Uncovers conflicts of interest & family enrichment.  

## Features
✅ **Real-time AI-driven tracking of financial corruption**  
✅ **Public corruption scores for politicians (1-100)**  
✅ **Automated alerts for shady financial activity**  
✅ **Blockchain-backed government spending transparency**  
✅ **Journalist & watchdog-ready reports**  

## How It Works
1. **Collects data** from SEC filings, campaign finance reports, government contracts, and leaked financial records.  
2. **Analyzes patterns** using AI, forensic accounting, and network mapping.  
3. **Flags corruption risks** based on detected conflicts of interest and financial gains.  
4. **Publishes findings** via a **public dashboard & automated reports**.
